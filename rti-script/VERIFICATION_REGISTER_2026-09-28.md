## N. Source reliability note

**`righttoinformation.wiki`** appears throughout the review and is the origin of at least one
demonstrable error (the "24 bodies" Second Schedule count — see X4). Its CIC-pendency page cites
*"Section 19(7) BNSS 2024"*; the BNSS is the Bharatiya Nagarik Suraksha Sanhita, a criminal
procedure code, and has no bearing on RTI appeals. Every figure in this register sourced only to
that site is marked **SECONDARY** and should be spoken with "approximately" or replaced from the
CIC's own MIS report and the state RTI portals.

**Preferred sources for the final read:** the Act itself; DoPT notifications; the CIC's MIS reports
and annual reports; State Information Commission annual reports; High Court and Supreme Court
orders; and established press (The Hindu, The Indian Express, Times of India, Economic Times,
Moneylife, Bar & Bench, LiveLaw, Supreme Court Observer).
